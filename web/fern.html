Drag a corner to move, scale, rotate, skew or flip a box. The solid dot is the corner that the frame’s solid dot maps to. Click a box to select it; press Delete to remove it. The frame stays fixed while you edit; “Fit view” resizes it to the current drawing.

Adding or dragging a map sets every p from its box’s area (|ad − bc|, with a small minimum), so new maps get enough points to show up. You can still type p by hand; values are rescaled to sum to 1. Corners can’t be dragged so far that a box stops being smaller than the frame, since the drawing would then fly apart.
